## What it is

Operations covers making reports fast, keeping data fresh and promoting changes safely.

## Why it exists

A model that works on an analyst's laptop may fail under real users, real data sizes and governed release processes.

## How it works

**Performance Analyzer** (View ribbon) times each visual's DAX, visual display and other work. Copy the query into DAX Studio to investigate further.

Optimization order is: remove unnecessary columns and rows, reduce cardinality, build a star schema, simplify measures, and limit visuals per page.

| Refresh | Shared capacity | Premium, PPU or Fabric |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduled refreshes per day | 8 | 48 |
| Duration limit | Under 2 hours | 5 hours (XMLA endpoint can bypass it) |

A full refresh can need about twice the model's memory, because old and new copies exist together. **Incremental refresh** partitions data and only reloads recent periods.

**Workspaces** hold content, and **deployment pipelines** promote it through development, test and production stages.

## Syntax and key APIs

A refresh pipeline can be triggered through the REST API:

```text
POST https://api.powerbi.com/v1.0/myorg/groups/{groupId}/datasets/{datasetId}/refreshes
```

## Simple example

A page takes 12 seconds. Performance Analyzer shows one matrix measure consuming 9 seconds. Replacing a `FILTER` over the whole fact with a column filter, and removing high-cardinality fields from the visual, brings the page under 2 seconds.

## Industry examples

Enterprises separate dev, test and production workspaces, version models in PBIP format with Git integration, and monitor refresh failures.

## Patterns and best practices

- Reduce high-cardinality columns such as raw timestamps.
- Avoid many visuals loading at once.
- Use incremental refresh for large history.
- Use the Apply button on slicers for expensive pages.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Aggregation tables accelerate DirectQuery. Direct Lake reduces refresh time on Fabric. Warehouse pre-aggregation can lower model complexity.

## Common mistakes

- Optimizing DAX before removing unneeded data.
- Scheduling all refreshes for the same hour.
- Publishing straight to production.
- Importing unneeded GUID columns.

## Interview questions

<Question q="How would you diagnose a slow visual?">Use Performance Analyzer to determine if the time is in DAX, visual rendering or a DirectQuery source, then investigate the query.</Question>

<Question q="Why might a refresh run out of memory?">A full refresh keeps both old and new data until completion.</Question>
