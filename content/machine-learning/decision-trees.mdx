## What it is

A decision tree predicts by asking a sequence of questions about features, ending at a leaf.

## Why it exists

Trees handle nonlinearity and interactions, need no scaling, and small ones are simple to explain.

## How it works

At each node, the algorithm chooses the split that most reduces impurity. Classification uses Gini or entropy, and regression uses variance reduction. Growth stops via depth, minimum samples or pruning.

Fully grown trees memorize the data, and they're unstable: a small data change can give a different tree.

## Intuition and formula

<Formula>Gini = 1 − Σ pₖ²</Formula>

<Formula>Entropy = − Σ pₖ log₂ pₖ</Formula>

## Worked example

A node with 40% churners has Gini 1 − 0.16 − 0.36 = 0.48, and entropy 0.971 bits. A split that produces two equally sized children with 10% and 70% churn has weighted Gini of 0.5 × 0.18 + 0.5 × 0.42 = 0.30, a reduction of 0.18.

## Python

```python
import math
from sklearn.datasets import make_classification
from sklearn.model_selection import cross_val_score
from sklearn.tree import DecisionTreeClassifier

gini = lambda p: 1 - p**2 - (1 - p) ** 2
assert round(gini(0.4), 2) == 0.48
assert round(-(0.4 * math.log2(0.4) + 0.6 * math.log2(0.6)), 3) == 0.971
assert round(gini(0.4) - (0.5 * gini(0.1) + 0.5 * gini(0.7)), 2) == 0.18

X, y = make_classification(n_samples=800, flip_y=0.2, random_state=0)
deep = DecisionTreeClassifier(random_state=0).fit(X, y)
limited = cross_val_score(DecisionTreeClassifier(max_depth=3, random_state=0), X, y, cv=5).mean()
unlimited = cross_val_score(DecisionTreeClassifier(random_state=0), X, y, cv=5).mean()

assert deep.score(X, y) == 1.0
assert limited > unlimited
```

## In industry

Shallow trees are used for rules and segmentation that business teams can act on, and trees form the base of ensembles.

## Assumptions and limitations

Boundaries are axis-aligned, and trees can't extrapolate beyond training targets. Impurity-based feature importance is biased toward high-cardinality features.

## Common mistakes

- Unlimited depth.
- Trusting impurity importances.
- Assuming a single tree is stable.
- Using trees for trends beyond the training range.

## Alternatives

Random forests and boosting greatly improve accuracy, and permutation importance gives better feature attribution.
