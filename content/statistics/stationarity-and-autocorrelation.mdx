## What it is

A series is **stationary** when its statistical properties don't change over time. **Autocorrelation (ACF)** is the correlation of a series with its own past. **Partial autocorrelation (PACF)** removes the effect of shorter lags. **Differencing** replaces a value with its change from a previous one.

## Why it exists

Many forecasting models assume stationarity, and non-stationary series produce spurious correlations. Two unrelated upward trends can show r > 0.9.

## How it works

| Test | H₀ | Reject means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Augmented Dickey-Fuller | Has a unit root (non-stationary) | Evidence of stationarity |
| KPSS | Stationary | Evidence of non-stationarity |

They have opposite nulls, so using both is informative.

Reading patterns:

| Process | ACF | PACF |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AR(p) | Tails off | Cuts off after p |
| MA(q) | Cuts off after q | Tails off |

First differencing removes a linear trend. Seasonal differencing at lag s removes a stable seasonal pattern. Over-differencing adds noise.

## Intuition and formula

<Formula>ρₖ = Cov(yₜ, yₜ₋ₖ) / Var(yₜ)</Formula>

<Formula>∇yₜ = yₜ − yₜ₋₁</Formula>

## Worked example

For the monthly series with a trend, ADF's p is 0.987 and KPSS's p is at most 0.01, so both indicate non-stationarity. After first differencing, ADF's p is effectively 0. Seasonal differencing at lag 12 gives ADF p = 0.0015.

## Python

```python
import warnings
import numpy as np
import pandas as pd
from statsmodels.tsa.stattools import acf, adfuller, kpss

rng = np.random.default_rng(11)
n = 120
y = pd.Series(200 + 1.5 * np.arange(n) + 25 * np.sin(2 * np.pi * np.arange(n) / 12) + rng.normal(0, 6, n))

assert adfuller(y)[1] > 0.9
assert adfuller(y.diff().dropna())[1] < 0.001
with warnings.catch_warnings():
    warnings.simplefilter("ignore")
    assert kpss(y, nlags="auto")[1] <= 0.01

seasonal_lag = acf(y.diff().dropna(), nlags=12)[12]
assert seasonal_lag > 0.4
```

## In industry

Stationarity checks are part of classical forecasting workflows. Machine-learning forecasters still use lag features and differencing.

## Assumptions and limitations

Formal tests have limited power on short series, and structural breaks can masquerade as non-stationarity.

## Common mistakes

- Reversing the conclusions of ADF and KPSS.
- Differencing repeatedly until a test passes.
- Correlating trending series.
- Forgetting to undo differencing when reporting forecasts.

## Alternatives

Log transforms stabilize variance, and detrending by regression suits deterministic trends. State-space models can handle non-stationarity directly.
